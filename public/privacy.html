Privacy

These notes cover the website. Read the iPhone app privacy notes for the app.

Updated September 16, 2026

No account needed. We don’t sell your data or use it to target ads.

Saved on your device

Your puzzle progress and preferences stay in this browser so you can return to your game. Clearing site data removes them. We don’t upload your saved board.

Basic play analytics

We use PostHog’s US Cloud to measure visits, traffic sources, clicks, moves, hints, results and play time to improve the game. It runs by default, using connection information to estimate visits without saving a tracking ID in your browser.

You can turn it off below or in Menu → Settings. We honor previous refusals, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. Turning it off stops new play analytics; it doesn’t erase past events or stop hosting statistics.

More about analytics and storage

PostHog combines your IP address, browser information and hostname with a daily changing secret, then removes the raw IP and user agent before storing events. We disable location enrichment, person profiles and session replay. These daily estimates can merge visitors and cannot reliably measure return visits across days.

The free PostHog plan provides a one-year analytics window, not a guaranteed deletion deadline; its privacy policy and retention rules apply. Earlier analytics collected with permission used random browser IDs. We keep that history separate and remove old browser IDs where possible.

We send no saved boards, names, emails or feedback text to PostHog. Your saved game includes your board, moves, hints, solve time, completion state and last play date. Your analytics choice may also be saved locally; we show a warning if saving fails. Blockers and connection failures can prevent measurement.

Private feedback

Your note and puzzle date are stored by here.now, readable only by the site owner, not other visitors. Avoid personal or sensitive information. Failed sends keep your note on screen, not in browser storage. Our policy is to delete raw feedback and legacy play counts within 30 days after receipt, keeping summaries.

Hosting statistics

here.now receives ordinary request information, including IP addresses, and reports aggregate paths, referrers and countries using daily visitor hashes. This runs independently of play analytics. It says it uses no tracking cookies and shows us no raw IP addresses. See here.now’s privacy policy.

Ads and questions

No ads currently. We’ll update this notice and any required choices before adding them. For privacy questions or removal requests, use Menu → Feedback in the game.
