Play analytics

On by default. To improve NookGrid, our US analytics provider receives opens, moves, hints, results, play time, errors and connection information linked to a random installation ID.

Turn off Play analytics in Settings to stop new events.

Data details

Our provider’s privacy and retention policies apply. A one-year reporting window isn’t a deletion deadline. Opting out doesn’t erase past events.
