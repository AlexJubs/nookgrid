Saved progress and streak recovery

Play works offline. Progress, hints, elapsed time and preferences are saved on your device. Deleting the app can remove unsynced saves.

In builds with streak protection enabled, your existing iCloud account can recover synced streak history after reinstalling or replacing a device. No NookGrid signup is required. Recovery depends on iCloud being available; continuing the same verified history also needs its anonymous credential in iCloud Keychain.

Streak verification sends a random player ID, puzzle completion and device integrity proof to our verification service. The service stores accepted puzzle dates and its receipt times to prevent fabricated streak records. Older history and offline completions remain local history until accepted. This is separate from optional Play analytics. In builds without streak protection, history stays on the device.

Play analytics

On by default. To improve NookGrid, our US analytics provider receives opens, moves, hints, results, play time, errors and connection information linked to a random installation ID.

Turn off Play analytics in Settings to stop new events.

Ads

Ads may appear during play and after a completed puzzle. Ad privacy choices, when available in Settings, are separate from Play analytics. Report an ad through Feedback.

Ad data details

Advertising can use connection information, approximate location, app or device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics. We request nonpersonalized ads. The advertising provider’s privacy policy explains its data use. Play analytics also measures ad outcomes and reported revenue when enabled.

Data details

Our provider’s privacy and retention policies apply. A one-year reporting window isn’t a deletion deadline. Opting out doesn’t erase past events.
