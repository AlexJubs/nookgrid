iPhone app privacy

Updated September 17, 2026

No account or ads. We don’t sell your data or use it to target ads.

Saved on your device

Your boards, hints, results and preferences stay on this device. They work offline and aren’t uploaded. Uninstalling the app removes these saves.

Play analytics

PostHog’s US Cloud measures moves, hints, results and play time to help improve the game. A random app installation ID lets us measure repeat play. It isn’t your Apple ID or an advertising ID, and we don’t use it to follow you across other apps.

Analytics run by default. You can turn them off in the app’s Preferences. Turning them off stops new events; it doesn’t delete past events.

Data details

We send no saved boards, names, email addresses or feedback text to PostHog. We disable location enrichment, person profiles and session replay. PostHog receives ordinary network connection information when processing requests. Reinstallation can create a new installation ID, so repeat-play counts estimate installations rather than people.

PostHog’s privacy policy and retention rules apply. Its one-year analytics window is not a guaranteed deletion deadline.

Feedback and questions

Feedback opens your email app. Nothing is sent until you send it. For privacy questions or deletion requests, email redpod22+nookgrid@gmail.com. The website has separate hosting and privacy practices.
