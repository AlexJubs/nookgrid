How to play

Arrange all nine places on the 3×3 grid to match the neighborhood plan. A new puzzle arrives daily.

The basics

  • Drag a place, or tap it then a square. Occupied squares swap places.
  • Follow the whole plan. A check marks a match with the current layout; those places may still need to move.
  • Undo reverses a move. Hint fixes one place in its correct square and counts in your result.
  • Reset restarts your timer and keeps hinted places fixed until you solve. Reset after solving clears every place and your hint count.

Reading the plan

  • Directly left: the next square to the left, in the same row.
  • Above: any higher row in the same column.
  • Touches sides: shares an edge, not a diagonal.

Keyboard: Tab moves focus; Enter or Space selects.
